# Local Market Agent 0.5.2

2026-11-26 · [简体中文](https://github.com/ThineLord/local-market-agent/blob/v0.5.2/docs/releases/0.5.2.zh-CN.md)

This patch corrects paper-trade reporting and replay, historical evidence checks, and parsing of public market data. It covers PRs #24–#32.

- **Paper orders and audit.** Retrying an intent with a verifiable execution receipt returns its original order, including after settlement or a newer quote. If several intents share a signal, workflow status identifies the intent that owns the order. When a legacy ledger lacks the receipt needed to distinguish them, it shows `unavailable` and offers `paper-status`. Daily reports count the recorded opening fee and closing fills once. The dashboard follows the order’s own risk decision even after it falls outside the recent activity list.
- **Read-only inspection.** `workflow-status`, `paper-status`, and `paper-replay` can inspect an incomplete ledger or a missing path without creating or migrating a database. Snapshot queries and chronological JSONL exports account for UTC offsets in existing timestamps.
- **Evidence and historical reports.** Saved watchlist briefs validate the identity and timing of each evidence item. Historical replay uses the observed market status until settlement becomes public, so an open market passes the market-status check at the correct time. A historical daily report uses only rows available by its cutoff. Paper-profit claims require eligible data and credible settlement for the selected market.
- **Public feeds and racing.** Public adapters handle missing market IDs, quoted labels, encoded searches, invalid prices and pagination, and preserve zero quotes. Racing replay keeps valid settled tickets visible when a sibling remains unknown and leaves incomplete meeting returns open. Racing research continues to report NO BET.

Upgrade from the repository root with `git pull --ff-only` and `python -m pip install -e ".[dev]"`. Existing databases require no migration. Consumers of workflow status should handle `unavailable` for ambiguous old records. Approval is still required for each ordinary paper order. See the [changelog](https://github.com/ThineLord/local-market-agent/blob/v0.5.2/docs/CHANGELOG.md) for individual cases.
