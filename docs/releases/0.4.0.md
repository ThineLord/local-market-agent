# Local Market Agent 0.4.0

Released: 2026-11-09 · [简体中文](https://github.com/ThineLord/local-market-agent/blob/v0.4.0/docs/releases/0.4.0.zh-CN.md)

This version connects real public-market depth discovery to reproducible NBA probability estimates and the existing paper workflow. It resolves the previous missing-depth/missing-probability bottleneck while preserving every existing risk and approval gate.

## Changes

- Bounded NBA/NFL game-market discovery ranks current best-quote USD buy capacity. Unknown, insufficient or legacy aggregate-only depth cannot qualify.
- A frozen NBA Elo model uses public final scores and home/neutral status, with prior-result lag and seasonal regression. Prices are not model inputs. All 120 source files have receipts and hashes; duplicate games and season coverage are verified.
- Chronological scoring on 1,230 held-out games reports Brier 0.209639 versus the fixed warmup home-rate baseline 0.247153; logloss 0.608033 versus 0.687444; ECE 0.028186. Paired day-bootstrap Brier difference: [-0.047430, -0.028255]. These are retrospective forecast metrics, not a trading backtest.
- Current NBA packets reproduce exact pregame identity, contract payout/rules/settlement source, quote, model state, validation and conservative support from their local source files. A source hash alone cannot establish semantic correctness.
- Preparation, approval and execution additionally enforce game start, event/history freshness, model/bin/cost consistency and the conservative net-edge threshold. A high point estimate or support fraction cannot override a failed prerequisite.

Functional PRs: [#15](https://github.com/ThineLord/local-market-agent/pull/15), [#16](https://github.com/ThineLord/local-market-agent/pull/16), [#17](https://github.com/ThineLord/local-market-agent/pull/17), plus the final NO-valuation correction. Codex implemented; subagents independently reviewed and performed guarded merges after exact-head CI. No bots were delegated work.

## Real-data acceptance

The first bounded NBA scan found 70 markets, 31 meeting depth/quote checks, 26 exact pregame matches and two supported candidates. One packet was selected, avoiding duplicate exposure. LAC's fixed-model probability was 0.669838 with 219 holdout examples in its home-probability bin. At a simulated fill price of 0.481, its cost-adjusted stress lower edge was 0.112352 versus 0.08 required. The paired NOP contract expresses the same exposure through BUY_NO. Quotes and capacities are time-bound observations.

The final refreshed scan found 70 markets, 31 depth/quote passes, 25 exact pregame matches and three supported contracts. The selected NOP BUY_NO packet had conservative depth $823.7048. Independent research review and portfolio checks passed; after the user's explicit approval of the exact intent, the isolated ledger filled $100 at 0.481 for 207.9002079 contracts plus $0.10 simulated fees. Repeated execution kept exactly one order/fill; content/link replay returned `ok=true`. The position remains open pending the real game's outcome, with no settlement fabricated.

SAC remained blocked: its point net edge was about 20.69%, but the stress lower edge was only 7.87%, below the unchanged 8% requirement. Expired observations and started-game fixtures also remain blocked. See the [detailed acceptance record](https://github.com/ThineLord/local-market-agent/blob/v0.4.0/docs/reports/0.4.0-validation.zh-CN.md) for final preparation/approval status and artifacts.

A final real-UI check exposed and fixed NO-position valuation using YES probabilities. Explicit market marks now invert for BUY_NO, while missing-mark cost fallback stays in the held outcome's units. The actual fill was unchanged. A separately labelled historical replay copy corrects the derived equity from $10,009.0476 to $9,998.6526 (unrealized -$1.3474), with all 14 non-derived business tables unchanged and execution replay still valid. The original database is preserved. Existing persisted NO equity/baseline records are not silently migrated; they need reconstruction from source observations in a separate reviewed replay before reuse.

## Validation and upgrade

500 offline tests passed, one optional network test skipped by default; the separately enabled public network test passed. The isolated synthetic 15-step CLI regression completed approval, fill, idempotent retry, replay and settlement. Those synthetic fills are not public-market results. Ruff, release consistency and diff checks passed.

Check out `v0.4.0`, then run `python -m pip install -e ".[dev]"`. No database or snapshot-schema migration. Existing sealed shapes are preserved; NBA proof is optional evidence metadata. The new collectors are explicit network opt-ins and require new output directories. Read the [probability protocol and commands](https://github.com/ThineLord/local-market-agent/blob/v0.4.0/docs/NBA_PROBABILITY.md).

Paper-only. The model ignores injuries, lineups and roster changes; residual-transfer assumptions may fail for an individual game. Both derived analyses share ESPN data. Edge-support fraction is not win probability; local checksums are not publisher signatures. Current games are unsettled: no future outcome or public settlement was invented. Profitability and production readiness remain unestablished.
